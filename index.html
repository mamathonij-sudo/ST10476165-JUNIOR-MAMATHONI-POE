About Us

DZUVHA Electrical Solution is a black owned company founded in 2019 that provides electrical installations, maintenance, solar solutions, electric fencing and electrical safety education to rural communities. It helps educate people about the use of electricity and how to use it safely to avoid damages such as fire, shock and burning of cables or houses. It is a highly recognized and recommended electrical company that serves the community and reduces unemployment by hiring young people to work.

Mission

Provide safe, affordable and reliable electrical services while educating rural communities, and teach youth how to deal with electricity and use it safely in everyday life.

Vision

Become the leading rural electrical solutions provider in South Africa and one of the most affordable, reliable electrical companies in South African communities, while giving youth the opportunity to work and learn about electricity.
